# Visual assets

The renderer loads two seamless, CC0 texture assets through `skins.ts`. They
are presentation-only: physics recipes remain IDs, particles, springs, and
faces, so any skin can be exchanged without changing simulation behavior.

| File | Source | License | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `stone-wall.jpg` | [RPG Stone Wall Texture — ForKotLow](https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-stone-wall-texture) | CC0 | bramble and masonry soft-body skin |
| `stylized-rock.png` | [Simple Stylized Toon Seamless Rock — Feraly](https://opengameart.org/content/simple-stylized-seamless-rock-or-stone-texture) | CC0 | moss courier and lab soft-body skin |

The source pages state that both textures are seamless and CC0. Attribution is
not required, but the source and author are retained here for provenance.
